I made this game using the guide and by the help of the teachers
It is a 30-second arcade clicker made with p5.js.
A lion jumps around a starry sky. Click it to score.
Hit it again within 2 seconds to build a combo.
Click or tap the lion as fast as you can.
Each hit is worth your current combo count (hit 1 = 1 point, hit 2 = 2, and so on).
If 2 seconds pass without a hit, the combo resets.
When the timer hits 0, your final score appears.
Click Play Again, or press Space or R, to restart.
